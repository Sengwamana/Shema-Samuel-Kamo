About Teo Creations

I'm Teo. I take raw footage and cut it into stories people actually stop scrolling for, content that informs, entertains and inspires.

I'm Theogene Shumbusho, a video editor and motion designer based in Kigali, Rwanda. From commercials and social campaigns to documentaries and YouTube, every cut, sound and frame has one job: keep your audience watching until the very last second.

How we work

From idea to final cut.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Tell me about your brand, your audience and the goal. A quick call or a message is enough to start.

  2. 02

    Script & edit

    I shape the story, write the hook, then cut the footage and add motion, sound and colour.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    You review it, we fine-tune together, and you get files ready for Reels, TikTok, YouTube or wherever it's going.

Beyond the edit

Every sketch is another way to tell a story.

Away from the timeline, I draw pencil portraits, signed teoArt. Drawing trains the same eye I bring to every edit: light, framing and the small details that make a face, or a story, feel real.
